## Research

I’m pursuing an MSc in Computer Science at Cairo University. My research examines how developers refactor Swift in practice and how to evaluate tools that detect those changes.

I co-authored [*Refactoring Swift: A Catalog of 10 Essential Practices for Code Improvement*](https://doi.org/10.1002/smr.70180), published in the *Journal of Software: Evolution and Process*. The study draws on changes in open-source Swift projects and feedback from Swift developers.
